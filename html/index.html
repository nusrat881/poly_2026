Bangladesh is a land of extraordinary and peaceful natural beauty. The country is crisscrossed by hundreds of flowing rivers like the Padma, Meghna, and Jamuna. Its landscape features lush green fields, golden crops, and six changing seasons. In the north and east, Sylhet shines with rolling green tea gardens and beautiful hills. In the south, the Sundarbans is a massive mangrove forest that is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.
